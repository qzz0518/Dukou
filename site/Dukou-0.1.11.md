## 更新日志

- 快捷微信转发新增「HTML 预览」。勾选后，导出的 ZIP 里会多一个 `index.html`，完整解压后双击就能像聊天界面一样看记录：搜索昵称和正文、按日期跳转、放大图片、直接播放音视频。
- 预览会自动把你自己的消息放在右侧；认不出来时，可以在右上角手动选。

## Changelog

- Quick WeChat forward can now include an HTML preview. Tick it and the ZIP gains an `index.html`: unpack the whole ZIP, open it, and read the chat as bubbles — search by sender or text, jump by date, zoom images, play audio and video inline.
- Your own messages are placed on the right automatically, and there is a picker in the top corner when they cannot be detected.

## 更新日志

- 快捷转发页重新排版：选项更紧凑，「开始」按钮固定在窗口底部。
- 新增续聊：同一个群再次发送时，自动提示只处理上次之后的新消息；每个群可以记住自己的 Prompt。
- 快捷微信转发存到文件夹时可选 Markdown，得到笔记加附件的文件夹，可直接放进 Obsidian。

## Changelog

- The quick forward pages are laid out afresh: tighter options, and the Start button stays at the bottom of the window.
- New: Resume. Sending the same chat again asks for only the messages after the last export, and each chat can remember its own prompt.
- Saving a quick WeChat forward to a folder can produce Markdown: a note with its attachments, ready for an Obsidian vault.

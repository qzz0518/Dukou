## 更新日志

- 快捷微信转发中途停下后，可以在设置里从中断处继续，接着导出更早的消息，已导出的部分会一起送到。
- 右键某条消息没有弹出「多选」时，改从相邻消息进入多选，不再直接停止。
- 进度浮窗挡住要点的位置时会自动让开；系统通知挡住微信时会等它消失。

## Changelog

- When a quick WeChat forward stops partway, you can continue it from Settings: Dukou exports the older messages from where it stopped and sends them along with what was already exported.
- When right-clicking a message brings up no “多选”, Dukou now enters multi-select through the message next to it instead of stopping.
- The progress capsule moves out of the way of a click, and Dukou waits for a notification banner covering WeChat to go away.

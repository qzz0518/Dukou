# Dukou 0.1.18

## New

- One entry per app, with the app's own icon: add any app in Settings → Entries and WeChat's Choose an App on Your Mac gains a "Send to …" item that sends straight to it.

## Improvements

- Send to Codex and Send to Claude now show ChatGPT's and Claude's icons; Send to Custom is replaced by these per-app entries, and the apps already in your list appear in the menu after the update.
